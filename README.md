At Jazeera University, computer laboratories are essential for supporting hands-on learning for Information Technology (IT) students. However, the current method of reporting laboratory problems is mostly manual. Students usually report issues verbally to instructors or lab assistants or wait until the problem is noticed. This approach often leads to delayed responses, poor documentation, and difficulty in tracking repeated or unresolved technical issues.
The proposed JU Smart Lab Management System provides a simple and efficient way for students to report laboratory problems during lab sessions. All reported issues are stored in a centralized database, allowing lab assistants and administrators to track, manage, and resolve problems in an organized manner. This helps improve communication, reduce system downtime, and ensure that laboratory issues are addressed more effectively
